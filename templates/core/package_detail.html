Field Naturalist Advice for {{ package.title }}

We recommend being ready in your hotel lobby 15 minutes before the pickup window. At Palatupana gate, arriving early ensures optimal queue placement for morning gate opening at 06:00 AM. Bring a light dust scarf or neck gaiter, polarized sunglasses, and a telephoto lens (100–400mm or equivalent). Our drivers respect official DWC buffer distances to ensure ethical, disturbance-free wildlife observation.
